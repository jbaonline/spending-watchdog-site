Privacy Policy

Spending Watchdog · Last updated 2 September 2026

Spending Watchdog (“the app”) helps you understand your spending by securely connecting to your financial accounts and automatically categorizing your transactions. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used and protected, and the choices you have.

The app is operated by Infraxeon (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy or your data: hello@infraxeon.com.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Your email address and an encrypted password credential, used solely to authenticate you.
  • If you enable two-factor authentication, a secret used to verify your authenticator codes.
  • A device push token, if you allow notifications, so we can send you renewal alerts.

Financial information (via Plaid)

When you choose to link a bank account, we use Plaid Inc. to establish that connection. Plaid collects your banking credentials directly through its own secure interface — we never see, receive, or store your bank username or password. Plaid’s handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

Through Plaid, we receive and store:

  • Account details: institution name, account name, type, mask (last digits), and current balance.
  • Transaction history: date, amount, merchant or description, currency, pending status, and category.
  • Recurring transaction streams, used to detect subscriptions and predict renewal dates.

Information you enter

  • Manual cash entries, custom categories, category corrections, a monthly budget, and alert preferences.

How we use your information

  • To display your spending, income, budget progress, subscriptions, and net worth inside the app.
  • To categorize transactions automatically and to apply the corrections you make.
  • To detect recurring charges and notify you before a subscription renews.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.

We do not sell your personal or financial information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or marketers, and we do not use your financial data for advertising or profiling.

How your data is protected

  • All traffic between the app and our servers, and between our servers and Plaid, is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase, hosted on Amazon Web Services) that is encrypted at rest, with encrypted backups.
  • Plaid access tokens receive additional application-layer encryption (AES-256-GCM) before storage, with keys held in a secrets manager and available only to server-side functions.
  • Every data table enforces row-level security, so your records are accessible only to your authenticated account.
  • Bank connections are handled exclusively server-side; the app on your device never holds a bank access token.
  • Incoming webhooks from Plaid are cryptographically verified before they are processed.
  • Optional two-factor authentication is available and recommended.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors, each acting on our behalf:

  • Plaid — bank connectivity and transaction data.
  • Supabase (on Amazon Web Services) — authentication, database, and server functions.
  • Expo — delivery of push notifications.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your data is retained while your account is active.
  • Unlinking a bank in the app revokes our access at Plaid and deletes the stored access token.
  • You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing hello@infraxeon.com. Deleting your account permanently removes your linked accounts, transactions, categories, and preferences.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to withdraw consent for bank connections at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address above; you can also disconnect any linked institution directly in the app at any time.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
