Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Application: Spending Watchdog Version: 1.0 Effective: 2 September 2026 Next review: 2 September 2027 Owner: Founder, Infraxeon

1Purpose and scope

This policy defines how long Spending Watchdog retains personal and financial data, the circumstances under which that data is deleted, and the technical controls that enforce those outcomes. It covers all consumer data processed by the application, including data received from the Plaid API, data entered by users, and account and authentication records.

Infraxeon operates the application. The founder is accountable for this policy, for its enforcement, and for its periodic review.

2Principles

  • Purpose limitation. Data is collected and retained only to provide the service the user asked for: categorized spending, subscription detection, budgeting, and net-worth tracking.
  • Minimization. No banking credentials are ever received or stored. No cardholder data, government identifiers, or payroll data are processed.
  • No secondary use. Consumer data is not sold, shared with advertisers or data brokers, or used for profiling or advertising.
  • Deletion on request. Users may terminate processing and have their data erased at any time.

4Deletion mechanisms and technical enforcement

  • Referential cascade. Every table containing consumer data carries a foreign key to the owning user account with ON DELETE CASCADE. Deleting the account deletes all dependent financial records in a single transaction; no orphaned data remains.
  • Third-party revocation. Unlinking an institution calls Plaid's /item/remove endpoint, terminating Plaid's authorization to access the account, before local credentials are destroyed.
  • Access isolation. Row-level security restricts every query to the authenticated user's own records, so deletion requests cannot affect, and cannot omit, another user's data.
  • Credential destruction. Access tokens are stored only as AES-256-GCM ciphertext; deletion removes the ciphertext, and the encryption key is held separately in a secrets manager.

5User rights and requests

Users may disconnect any linked financial institution at any time from within the application, which immediately revokes third-party access. Users may request erasure of their account and all associated data by contacting hello@infraxeon.com. Requests are actioned within 30 days of verification of the requester's identity, and confirmation is provided once completed. Users may also request access to, correction of, or export of their personal data through the same channel.

6Processors

Data is processed by a limited set of providers acting on Infraxeon's behalf: Plaid (bank connectivity and transaction data), Supabase on Amazon Web Services (authentication, database, server functions), and Expo (push notification delivery). Deletion performed in the application propagates to the primary data store; Plaid access is revoked through the API as described above.

7Legal compliance

This policy is written to support obligations under applicable consumer privacy laws, including rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability, and the principle that personal data is not retained longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Where a specific legal obligation requires longer retention of a record, that record will be retained only for the period required and then deleted.

8Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the policy owner, and additionally whenever the application begins processing a new category of data, adds or replaces a processor, or materially changes how data is stored. Each review is recorded in the version history below.
