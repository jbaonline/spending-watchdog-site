What it does

Automatic categorization

Transactions arrive already sorted into food, entertainment, subscriptions and more. Correct one and every charge from that merchant follows, past and future.

Subscription watchdog

Finds recurring charges hiding in your statement, shows what they cost you monthly, and sends a notification days before each renewal.

Budgets that stay quiet

One monthly budget, or a limit per category. The app only speaks up when you are drifting past it.

The whole picture

Balances, net worth over time, month-to-month trends, and cash you spend outside the bank — in one place.

How your data is handled

  • We never see your bank login. Credentials are entered directly with your bank through Plaid; they are never sent to, or stored by, this app.
  • Access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and readable only by server-side functions — never by the app on your device.
  • Your records are isolated. Row-level security means an authenticated session can only ever reach its own data.
  • Read-only. The app retrieves transactions and balances. It cannot move money.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. No advertisers, no data brokers, no use of your financial data for profiling.
  • You can leave cleanly. Disconnect a bank at any time — which revokes access at Plaid — or delete your account and all its data from inside the app.
